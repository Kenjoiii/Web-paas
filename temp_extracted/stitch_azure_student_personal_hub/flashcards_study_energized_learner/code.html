Question

Describe the mechanism and regioselectivity of the E1 reaction.

touch_app Click to reveal answer
Answer

The E1 mechanism is a two-step process:

  • 1

    Rate-determining formation of a carbocation intermediate.

  • 2

    Rapid loss of a beta-proton by a weak base.

  • star_rate

    Regioselectivity: Usually follows Zaitsev's rule, favoring the more substituted (stable) alkene.
